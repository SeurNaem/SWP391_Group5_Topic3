### Architecture Implemented

#### Role-Based Routing System
```
User Login → Role Detection → Route Direction
├── Admin Role → /dashboard/* (Admin Management System)
└── Staff Role → /staff/* (Staff Activity System)
```

#### Admin System (Preserved from VoTuanAnh)
**Routes:** `/dashboard/*`
**Access:** Admin role only
**Features:**
- 📊 Reports Dashboard (with charts and analytics)
- 👥 User Management (Staff, Drivers, Admins)
- 🔌 Charging Station Management
- 💳 Subscription Management
- 💰 Payment Integration

#### Staff System (Integrated from VoAnhKhoa)
**Routes:** `/staff/*`
**Access:** Staff role only
**Features:**
- 📱 Staff Dashboard (`/staff`)
- ⚡ Charging Points Management (`/staff/charging-points/:stationId`)
- 📊 Session History & Analytics (`/staff/session-history/:stationId`)
- 🔍 Session Detail Search (`/staff/session-detail`)
